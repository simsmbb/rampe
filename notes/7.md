```text id="m1jmxn"

```

That distinction is critical.

# What TextQL Actually Is

From their docs, TextQL’s ontology is a governed semantic layer for:

* business metrics
* entities
* joins
* dimensions
* consistent querying
* natural language analytics ([TextQL Docs][1])

Example:

```text id="pk8ct4"
Customer
Revenue
Orders
Region
Churn
```

with:

* relationships
* metric definitions
* SQL mappings

The ontology mainly helps:

* AI generate correct SQL
* users query data consistently
* governance of metrics and business logic
